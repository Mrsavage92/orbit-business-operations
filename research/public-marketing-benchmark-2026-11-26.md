# Public-source marketing benchmark: specialist automation and integration consultancies

Date: 26 November 2026. Public-source research only. This file records examples and transferable marketing mechanisms, not confidential company strategy, forecasts, client records or claims about competitor profitability.

## Evidence rules
- A published offer proves an offer exists, not that it sells.
- Directory reviews and externally confirmed engagements show market activity, not necessarily unique customers, revenue or profit.
- Interview revenue and acquisition claims are founder-reported, dated and unaudited.
- An observed marketing channel is not proof that it generated sales. Record unknown acquisition attribution as unknown.
- Public awards, customer outcomes and company revenue are distinct. Do not conflate them.
- Do not contact competitors under false pretences, submit dummy lead forms, collect personal data indiscriminately, or copy their content.

## Transferable marketing mechanisms
1. Partner distribution: one relationship can introduce several businesses. Vendor directories and existing professional associations provide different forms of access.
2. Education in existing communities: a useful demonstration or workshop can establish expertise without relying on a large owned audience.
3. Search-led answers: address a specific buyer problem or software handoff; use original examples and route to implementation help.
4. Useful free tools and templates: offer practical value first. Measure downstream enquiries rather than downloads alone.
5. Proof and referrals: use authorised customer outcomes and request introductions when appropriate.
6. Founder-led social: combine personal trust, practical education, relevant examples and periodic genuine invitations. Do not attach a pitch to every personal post.
7. Separate channel activity from conversion evidence. Do not assume advertising, cold outreach or organic visibility is successful merely because it exists.

## Follow-on research checklist
- For each comparator: inventory current social accounts and recent content formats, posting cadence, links and calls to action.
- Map exact publicly accessible journeys from a search result, workshop, partner directory or social post to a contact/booking page. Do not submit forms.
- Audit public ad libraries by region and advertiser name; record observed ads, dates, creative, destination and limits. No ads found does not establish no ad spend.
- Check estimated search visibility, ranking pages, relevant keywords and backlinks with a documented provider/date; estimates are not actual analytics.
- Verify award and event listings from organisers, and partnership directory presence from vendors.
- Look for recent founder interviews that describe lead source or revenue. Do not fill gaps with estimates of private turnover.
- Preserve observed evidence, dated sources, limitations and the distinction between research hypotheses and proven results.
