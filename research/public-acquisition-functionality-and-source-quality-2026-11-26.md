# Public-source acquisition functionality and source quality benchmark

Research checkpoint: 26 November 2026. General functional research only, based on published product and official source documentation. No private strategy, account information, internal test data, commercial records or operational configuration is included.

## Functional comparison

The publicly documented GojiBerry workflow separates an ideal customer profile, sourcing signals, qualification, enrichment, review, campaigns and reply handling. This is useful for understanding the categories of work in acquisition software, but does not disclose its underlying proprietary data, matching algorithms, licensed data coverage, provider agreements or measured conversion rates. A description of an advertised feature is not independent verification of its accuracy.

| Public capability | Published mechanism | Evidence limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agent and ICP configuration | Industry, role, company characteristics, exclusions and signals | Exact scoring and data completeness unknown |
| Company similarity | Lookalike matching against selected profiles or domains | Similarity establishes possible fit, not purchase intent |
| Technology indicators | Public job descriptions and website evidence | A technology mention is not proof of deployment or buying demand |
| Qualification and review | Priorities and review modes | Actual precision and false-positive rate unknown |
| Contact enrichment | Advertised multi-provider waterfall | Availability and legal permission to contact are separate questions |
| Reply handling | Inbox and response detection | Actual delivery, attribution and reply accuracy unverified |

## Public source types and what they establish

- [AusTender guidance](https://sellingtogov.finance.gov.au/faq/using-austender) and [buy NSW opportunity help](https://buy.nsw.gov.au/help/opportunities-hub): official opt-in notifications may identify procurement notices. Check actual stage, closing date, eligibility and permitted response route; proposed or expired notices are not open buying opportunities.
- [ABN Lookup web services](https://abr.business.gov.au/Tools/WebServices): registration and business identity evidence, not a buyer database or indication of demand. Follow registration and usage terms.
- [Lever public posting documentation](https://github.com/lever/postings-api) and [Greenhouse Job Board API](https://docs.greenhouse.io/job-board.html): published role descriptions can provide company context. An advertised job is not necessarily a request for a consulting supplier; a publicly accessible endpoint is not blanket permission for every commercial use.
- [Reddit Responsible Builder Policy](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/42728983564564-Responsible-Builder-Policy), [LinkedIn User Agreement](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement), and [ACMA spam guidance](https://www.acma.gov.au/avoid-sending-spam): check current platform and legal requirements. Do not bypass access restrictions, harvest private identities, or equate a public post or published address with consent to send messages.

## Evidence-quality checklist

Distinguish explicit purchasing requests, proposed procurement, invited partnership opportunities, company-level contextual indicators and irrelevant supplier advertisements. Preserve original references, observed dates, relevant expiry, eligibility, conflicting evidence, and reason for classification. Separate fit, intent, confidence and authorisation. Deduplicate stable opportunity identifiers while allowing distinct projects from the same company. Respect withdrawal, suppression and deletion requirements.

Technical success alone is not commercial success. Measure actual persisted outcomes, source coverage, false positives, time to useful review and eventual genuine conversations rather than raw scraped counts or job success statuses. Preserve the distinction between a historical reported metric, an independently reproduced test, an unverified assumption and a live operational result.
